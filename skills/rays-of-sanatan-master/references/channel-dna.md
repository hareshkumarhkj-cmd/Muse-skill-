# Channel DNA: Rays of Sanatan

## Accessible reference observations

The user showed Epic Yug's Popular tab: myth/history/mystery topics, temple
engineering, expressive mythological figures and high-view-count titles. The
supplied 169.733-second 720x1280/30fps video contains sampled scenes of large
stone construction, blue scale figures, temple towers, cyan joint diagrams,
map-like explainers and devotional imagery. The opening sequence changes
viewpoint and human poses; it is not just a still-image zoom.

Observed visual language: monumental scale, detailed materials, clear large
subjects, strong depth, golden light, cool shadows, cinematic contrast and
alternation between spectacle and explanatory detail. Some source frames look
synthetic/3D; the actual creation tools cannot be identified from pixels alone.
View counts do not prove why a video succeeded. A channel title is not factual
evidence. Source audio and every cut were not perceptually verified here.

## Original Rays direction (creative proposal)

- Promise: curiosity with dignity; accessible stories of art, history and
  living tradition, with an honest boundary between evidence and belief.
- Emotional arc: one real question → specific discovery → meaningful payoff.
  A 15s test should deliver one idea, not compress an entire documentary.
- Brand: midnight blue `#07121F`, warm gold `#F4CE83`, natural sandstone;
  cyan only for an intentional engineering/diagram highlight.
- Hindi-first, conversational and respectful. Do not make religious devotion
  sound like scientific proof. No contempt toward any belief/community.
- Visual hierarchy: one focal subject, believable scale, controlled highlights,
  generous mobile-safe caption area. Avoid random ornament as a substitute for
  relevant imagery. Let hero frames breathe.
- Audio direction WHEN available: clear, warm original Hindi storyteller;
  restrained cinematic/devotional atmosphere, minimal intentional SFX. This is
  a proposed direction, not an identification of the reference narrator/track.
- Closing: a question only when the story earns it; otherwise a memorable
  truthful close. No engagement bait or invented peril.

## Fixed vs adjustable

Keep originality, truthful attribution, mobile readability, exact delivery
specifications and capability honesty fixed. Adjust the palette, shot pace,
caption density, voice energy and music to the topic and current user request.
Do not impose a giant caption band on every production: the supplied samples
were often image-led. Captions must aid comprehension and never cover faces.

The first AI-image permission covered a particular original test. Inspect
the current brief before applying generation to new tasks. Do not repeatedly
seek approval for actions already authorized within the current scope.
