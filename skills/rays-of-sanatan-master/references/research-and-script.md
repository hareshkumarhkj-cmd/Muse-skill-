# Research and Hindi script

## Evidence workflow

1. List the material claims before writing the hook. Separate age, dimensions,
   location, construction technique, textual attribution and miracle stories.
2. Find and actually read sources. Prefer official archaeological/temple
   records, museum catalogues, primary inscriptions or attributed editions;
   use serious academic work for interpretations. Record source scope, not
   merely a URL. Websites can disagree and official sources can have limits.
3. For precise, disputed or sensational claims seek independent corroboration.
   If unavailable, narrow/attribute the wording or remove it. Two sites copying
   the same text are not two independent sources. Search snippets are leads.
4. Record title/author or institution, URL or publication details, section,
   access date, supported claim and uncertainty. Do not invent a citation.
   Without source access, produce an explicitly fictional proof or a draft
   flagged unverified; do not call it fact-checked.
5. Keep categories visible in planning and appropriate final wording:

| Category | Safe treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Documented fact | State only what the inspected source supports |
| Scriptural attribution | Name the text/tradition; do not claim historical verification |
| Local belief/legend | “लोकमान्यता है…” / “कहा जाता है…” |
| Disputed interpretation | Attribute whose view and what remains contested |
| Fictional visualization | Label it; avoid a real-world evidentiary implication |

Do not use “विज्ञान भी हैरान है”, “आज तक कोई समझ नहीं पाया”, an exact
stone weight or an invented curse merely because it makes a stronger hook.
Generated diagrams are illustrative unless produced from verified technical data.
An AI-image disclaimer does not fix a false narrated claim.

## Write for a speaker, not a poster

- Start with a specific question, contrast or image the video can answer.
- Use short Hindi sentences with natural word order and one thought per beat.
  Avoid unnecessarily Sanskritized vocabulary or forced English slogans.
- Keep a pronunciation sheet for deity/place names, compounds and numbers.
  Spell numerals as spoken words in the TTS input when that improves clarity.
- Separate three fields: final narration, on-screen caption, delivery notes.
  “[pause]” is a direction, not literal narration. Translate directions to the
  selected engine's supported syntax; do not assume SSML support.
- Avoid overclaiming architectural causality from a fabricated visual model.
  Fictional tests can say “यह एक काल्पनिक दृश्य है” in the script/disclosure.
- For 15s initially estimate 25–35 words including pauses. Generate/record a
  real take, measure, shorten and retry. Do not cram a long script at an
  unnatural speed to satisfy the estimate. Preserve the frame contract.

## Original 15s fictional exercise

Theme: stone balance, not a claim about a named historical temple.

Narration draft: “ये पत्थर टिके कैसे हैं? [short pause] हर जोड़ में छिपा
है संतुलन। छोटे जोड़, विराट रचना। कला या इंजीनियरिंग? [emphasis] दोनों।”

This is a writing/timing starting point, not a generated voice file or verified
speech duration. The supplied runnable example is silent and uses related
captions; narration must be created and measured before a voiced version exists.

## Script gate

Every sentence must have a role, fit an actual timed take and have an accurate
claim category. If a strong hook needs a false premise, change the hook.
