# Bundled assets

`public/hook.png`, `public/detail.png`, `public/hero.png` are original AI images
generated for this test after the user's explicit permission. The images depict
fictional architecture. They are not images extracted from Epic Yug's video.

Shared visual direction: vertical 9:16, cinematic photorealistic architectural
visualization, fictional South Indian sandstone temple, warm golden light,
midnight-blue shadows, tiny cobalt-blue human scale figures, detailed stone,
no text, logos or watermark. Shot directions:

- Hook: low-angle carved pillar and temple tower, monumental scale.
- Detail: close-up interlocking stone seam with cyan illustrative glow.
- Hero: wide three-quarter aerial temple view at sunset.

These are still images. All zooms, scanner graphics, particles, captions and
branding are rendered in code. The cyan line already present in the detail
image is an artistic visualization, not a measured structural annotation.

`public/fonts/NotoSansDevanagari-Bold.ttf` is an open-source system sans font
bundled for Hindi portability, from the environment's Noto font installation.
The accompanying upstream/Debian copyright and SIL OFL license notice is
included in `public/fonts/LICENSE.txt`.

No audio assets are used. No reference media is bundled.
